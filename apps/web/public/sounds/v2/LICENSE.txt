Voice announcements (7 WAV files listed in announcements.json)
Original product wording synthesized offline with Piper 1.4.1 (GPL-3.0 tool),
using en_US-ljspeech-medium from rhasspy/piper-voices. The model is trained from
scratch on the public-domain LJ Speech dataset, rather than a Lessac-derived model.
No voice model or Piper runtime is distributed with this application.

Voice model source and model card:
https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/main/en/en_US/ljspeech/medium
